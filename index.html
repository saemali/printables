How to run bingo at an office or family party

  1. One card per player. Every card is different, so print as many as you have guests. The free sample covers 4 players; for a bigger group you need more unique cards.
  2. Cut the call slips and put them in a hat or bowl. The caller draws one at a time and ticks it off on the caller sheet.
  3. Use markers that don't need pens: wrapped sweets, coins or small paper squares work well and keep the table tidy.
  4. Play several rounds with different goals: a single line, four corners, then a full card for the last round.
  5. Keep prizes small so it stays friendly: a chocolate bar, a mug, or first pick in the gift swap.

Printing tip: choose "Actual size" or 100% in the print dialog. Plain paper is fine; light card (160–200 gsm) makes reusable cards and sturdier tags.
